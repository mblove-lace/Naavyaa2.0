  • Indian Patterns

    • Indian cotton saree prints are more than just patterns on fabric — they are stories woven through generations. From traditional motifs to contemporary designs, each print tells a unique tale.

    Like,Kalamkari brings mythological tales and nature to life through intricate brushwork, while Hand Block Printing celebrates the skill of artisans who carve and stamp each motif by hand. Ajrakh, with its rich natural dyes and symmetrical patterns, reflects centuries of craftsmanship and patience.

    Together, these timeless arts become something you not only wear but also represents your heritage.

  • Everyday is ethnic day

    • Heritage is not just meant to keep in closet, rather it belongs in your everyday wardrobe.

    Got a meeting? Pair a subtle cotton saree with delicate Ajrakh prints for a look that's professional with just the right touch of personality.

    Running errands on a warm summer day? Slip into a lightweight printed kurti that's breathable, comfortable, yet as vibrant as you are.

    Heading out on a date? A graceful Batik print strikes the perfect balance between effortless and elegant.

    Celebrating a festival? Embrace the vibrant colors and intricate patterns of a traditional Kalamkari saree to truly embody the festive spirit.
